Reconstitution methodology

How published protocols describe preparing lyophilized powder into solution for laboratory use.

Procedure described in published protocols

  1. Hands are washed and the work surface cleaned with alcohol.
  2. The BAC water vial stopper is swabbed with an alcohol prep pad.
  3. The calculated BAC volume is drawn into a 3ml reconstitution syringe.
  4. The peptide vial stopper is swabbed with an alcohol prep pad.
  5. BAC water is directed down the inner wall of the vial — never onto the lyophilized powder.
  6. The vial is gently swirled until fully dissolved. It is not shaken.

Subcutaneous administration (research methodology)

How researchers administer subcutaneous injections to research subjects in laboratory settings.

⚠

This information describes laboratory research methodology. It is not instruction for human self-administration.

Reading the syringe

100 units = 1 mL. If your vial is 5 mg/mL, then 10 units = 0.5 mg, 20 units = 1.0 mg.

Administration technique (as described)

  • A fold of skin is pinched
  • Needle inserted at 45° (lean subjects) or 90° (average)
  • Plunger drawn slightly to aspirate — if blood appears, the site is changed
  • Solution injected slowly over 3–5 seconds
  • Needle withdrawn and disposed immediately in a sharps container

Rotation sites

  • Lower abdomen (2 in. from navel)
  • Outer thigh
  • Back of upper arm
  • Upper buttock

Sites are rotated daily to prevent lipohypertrophy.
