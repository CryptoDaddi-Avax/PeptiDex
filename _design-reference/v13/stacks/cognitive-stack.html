What is the Cognitive Stack?

The Cognitive Stack is a documented research combination for focus, memory, and neuroprotection. It pairs a stimulating neurotrophic nootropic, a calming anxiolytic, and a neuropeptide complex.

Semax raises BDNF and NGF to support plasticity, Selank balances that stimulation with anxiolysis so focus is calm rather than jittery, and Cerebrolysin adds broad neurotrophic-factor mimicry and neuroprotection.

Who is it for?

This is an intermediate-methodology combination, suited to researchers comfortable with intranasal administration and the included IV/IM neuropeptide complex.

What peptides are in the Cognitive Stack?

The Cognitive Stack contains Semax (600mcg/day), Selank (300mcg/day), and Cerebrolysin (5ml 5x/wk).
