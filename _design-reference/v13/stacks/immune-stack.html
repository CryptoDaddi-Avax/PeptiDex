What is the Immune Stack?

The Immune Stack is a documented research combination for immune resilience. It pairs a T-cell-maturing peptide with a broad-spectrum antimicrobial cathelicidin and an anti-inflammatory tripeptide.

Thymosin Alpha-1 restores balanced T-cell function, LL-37 adds frontline antimicrobial defense, and KPV dampens the excess inflammation that accompanies immune dysregulation — three complementary arms of immune support.

Who is it for?

This is an intermediate-methodology combination suited to researchers studying immune function who are comfortable with multi-component scheduling.

What peptides are in the Immune Stack?

The Immune Stack contains Thymosin Alpha-1 (1.6mg 2x/wk), LL-37 (100mcg/day), and KPV (500mcg/day).
