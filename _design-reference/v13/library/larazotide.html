What it does

Larazotide acetate (AT-1001) is an orally administered octapeptide that acts locally in the gut lumen. It is the most clinically advanced 'tight-junction regulator,' reaching Phase 3 study for celiac disease.

By antagonizing zonulin — the protein that loosens intestinal tight junctions — it helps preserve gut-barrier integrity and reduce the permeability associated with celiac disease and 'leaky gut.'

Mechanism

Larazotide antagonizes zonulin, the endogenous modulator that opens the tight junctions between intestinal epithelial cells. It also stabilizes the actin cytoskeleton and tight-junction proteins.

By keeping tight junctions closed, it reduces paracellular permeability and the passage of immunogenic antigens (such as gluten peptides), dampening the downstream inflammatory cascade.

Zonulin antagonism

Blocks zonulin signaling that loosens intestinal tight junctions.

Barrier integrity

Stabilizes tight-junction proteins to keep the gut barrier closed.

Reduced permeability

Limits passage of immunogenic antigens, dampening inflammation.
