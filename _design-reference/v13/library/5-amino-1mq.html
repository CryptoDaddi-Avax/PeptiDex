What it does

5-Amino-1-methylquinolinium (5-Amino-1MQ) is a small-molecule inhibitor of nicotinamide N-methyltransferase (NNMT), an enzyme that is overexpressed in white adipose tissue in obesity.

By inhibiting NNMT it preserves cellular nicotinamide and NAD+ pools and shifts adipocyte metabolism, producing reduced fat mass and improved metabolic markers in preclinical models.

Why researchers study it

Mechanism

5-Amino-1MQ blocks NNMT, the enzyme that methylates nicotinamide for excretion. Inhibiting it raises intracellular nicotinamide and the NAD+ salvage flux, while also relieving an NNMT-driven metabolic 'sink' in adipocytes.

The net effect is increased cellular energy expenditure and reduced fat storage, with preserved sirtuin activity downstream of higher NAD+.

NNMT inhibition

Blocks the methyltransferase overexpressed in obese adipose tissue.

NAD+ preservation
