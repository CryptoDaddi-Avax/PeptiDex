What it does

Cerebrolysin is a standardized peptide complex produced by enzymatic breakdown of purified porcine brain proteins, manufactured by Ever Pharma. It contains low-molecular-weight peptides and free amino acids that mimic the action of endogenous neurotrophic factors.

It is approved or used clinically in more than 60 countries for ischemic stroke, traumatic brain injury, and vascular dementia, and is administered as an intravenous or intramuscular course rather than a single injection.

Why researchers study it

  • Mimics endogenous neurotrophic factors (BDNF, GDNF)
  • Improved cognitive recovery in acute ischemic stroke (CASTA)
  • Studied for traumatic brain injury and vascular dementia
  • Used clinically in 60+ countries with a large trial base

Mechanism

Cerebrolysin's peptide fraction mimics the activity of neurotrophic factors such as BDNF and GDNF, supporting neuronal survival, differentiation, and synaptic maintenance after injury.

It also limits excitotoxicity and free-radical damage and supports neurogenesis, the combination underlying its study in stroke recovery and neurodegeneration.

Neurotrophic mimicry

Peptide fraction reproduces BDNF/GDNF-like signaling, supporting neuronal survival and plasticity.

Neuroprotection

Limits excitotoxicity and oxidative damage following ischemic or traumatic injury.

Neurorecovery

Supports neurogenesis and functional recovery in stroke and dementia research.
