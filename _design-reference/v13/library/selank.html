What it does

Selank is a synthetic heptapeptide based on the immunopeptide tuftsin, developed at the Institute of Molecular Genetics in Russia. It is studied as an anxiolytic that does not produce the sedation or dependence associated with benzodiazepines.

Alongside its calming effect, it shows nootropic and immunomodulatory activity, and influences the balance of monoamine neurotransmitters and BDNF expression.

Why researchers study it

  • Anxiolytic effect without sedation or dependence
  • Modulates GABAergic tone and BDNF expression
  • Stabilizes enkephalins, extending their action
  • Nootropic and immunomodulatory activity reported

Mechanism

Selank modulates the expression of GABA-related genes and stabilizes endogenous enkephalins by inhibiting their breakdown, producing an anxiolytic effect without direct receptor sedation.

It also influences the serotonin and dopamine systems and increases BDNF expression, contributing to its reported nootropic and mood-balancing effects.

GABAergic modulation

Modulates GABA-related gene expression for anxiolysis without sedation.

Enkephalin stabilization

Inhibits enzymatic breakdown of enkephalins, prolonging their action.

BDNF support

Raises BDNF expression, contributing to nootropic and mood effects.
