What it does

Gonadorelin is the synthetic form of gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH), the decapeptide that the hypothalamus uses to drive the pituitary release of LH and FSH.

When delivered in a pulsatile pattern that mimics natural secretion, it stimulates the HPG axis — supporting testicular function and fertility, including during testosterone-replacement protocols.

Mechanism

Gonadorelin binds the pituitary GnRH receptor on gonadotrope cells, triggering the release of luteinizing hormone (LH) and follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH).

Because GnRH signaling depends on a pulsatile rhythm, intermittent dosing stimulates the axis (whereas continuous exposure desensitizes it) — the basis for its use in supporting testicular function and fertility.
