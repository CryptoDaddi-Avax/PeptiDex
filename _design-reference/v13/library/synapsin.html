What it does

Synapsin is a compounded nootropic formulation combining ginsenoside Rg3 — a saponin from Panax ginseng — with an NAD precursor. It is delivered intranasally and used primarily in functional-medicine and traumatic-brain-injury research settings.

The rationale pairs Rg3's neuroprotective, anti-inflammatory signaling with NAD-based mitochondrial support, aiming to improve synaptic function and cognitive performance.

Mechanism

Ginsenoside Rg3 modulates neuroinflammatory signaling and supports synaptic plasticity, while the NAD precursor feeds mitochondrial NAD+ pools that power neuronal energy metabolism.

Together the components aim to improve bioenergetic capacity and synaptic function, the basis for the blend's use in cognitive-recovery research.

Neuroinflammation control

Ginsenoside Rg3 modulates inflammatory signaling in neural tissue.

Mitochondrial support

NAD precursor replenishes NAD+ pools fueling neuronal energy metabolism.

Synaptic plasticity

Combined action aims to support synaptic function and recovery.
