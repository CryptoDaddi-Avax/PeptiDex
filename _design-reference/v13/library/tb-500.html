What it does

TB-500 is a synthetic peptide corresponding to the active region of Thymosin Beta-4, a naturally occurring protein central to cell migration and tissue repair. Its defining biochemical action is the sequestration of actin, which mobilizes cells toward sites of injury.

It is studied for systemic wound healing, soft-tissue repair, and cardiovascular regeneration, and is frequently combined with BPC-157 in repair-focused research protocols.

Mechanism

TB-500 sequesters G-actin, regulating the actin cytoskeleton to enable cell migration, differentiation, and the recruitment of reparative and endothelial cells to damaged tissue.

Through this and pro-angiogenic signaling, it supports wound closure, soft-tissue repair, and — in cardiac models — improved function following infarction.

Cell migration

Sequesters actin to mobilize reparative and endothelial cells to injury sites.

Angiogenesis

Promotes new blood-vessel formation supporting tissue regeneration.

Cardiac repair
