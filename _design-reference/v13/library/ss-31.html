What it does

SS-31 (elamipretide) is a small cell-permeable tetrapeptide that concentrates in the inner mitochondrial membrane, where it binds cardiolipin — a phospholipid essential to the electron-transport chain.

By stabilizing cardiolipin, SS-31 helps preserve cristae structure and efficient ATP production while reducing reactive-oxygen-species leakage, the basis for its study in heart failure and mitochondrial disease.

Mechanism

SS-31 binds cardiolipin in the inner mitochondrial membrane, protecting the structural integrity of cristae and the supercomplexes of the electron-transport chain. This preserves coupling efficiency under stress.

The result is improved ATP synthesis and a marked reduction in oxidative stress, which underlies investigation across cardiac, renal, and neurodegenerative research.

Cardiolipin binding

Concentrates in the inner mitochondrial membrane and binds cardiolipin, stabilizing cristae architecture.

ROS reduction
