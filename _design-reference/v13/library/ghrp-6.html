What it does

GHRP-6 was one of the first synthetic growth-hormone-releasing peptides characterized. It is a hexapeptide ghrelin mimetic that releases GH while also producing pronounced appetite stimulation through the ghrelin pathway.

Its strong orexigenic effect distinguishes it from the more selective Ipamorelin and the more potent GHRP-2, and it remains a reference compound in GH-secretagogue research.

Mechanism

GHRP-6 binds the GHSR-1a receptor, prompting pituitary GH release and centrally stimulating appetite — both classic ghrelin-pathway effects.

Co-administration with a GHRH analog produces an additive GH pulse, while the orexigenic effect makes it of particular interest where appetite support is desired.

GH release

Activates GHSR-1a to release pituitary growth hormone.

Appetite stimulation

Engages central ghrelin circuits, markedly increasing hunger.

GHRH synergy

Combines additively with GHRH analogs for a larger GH pulse.
