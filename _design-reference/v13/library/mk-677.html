What it does

MK-677 (ibutamoren) is a non-peptide, orally bioavailable agonist of the ghrelin receptor (GHSR-1a). Unlike injectable GHRPs, it sustains GH and IGF-1 elevation around the clock from a single daily oral dose.

It was investigated by Merck for conditions including frailty and GH deficiency, and remains widely studied for its convenient, sustained effect on the GH/IGF-1 axis.

Why researchers study it

  • Oral administration — no injection or reconstitution
  • Sustained 24-hour GH/IGF-1 elevation
  • Long-duration human data (up to 12 months)
  • Non-peptide structure resists enzymatic breakdown

Mechanism

MK-677 mimics ghrelin at the GHSR-1a receptor, stimulating sustained pulsatile GH release and a durable rise in IGF-1 without the rapid clearance of peptide GHRPs.

Its oral bioavailability and ~24-hour half-life produce steady GH-axis stimulation, distinguishing it from injectable secretagogues that require multiple daily doses.
