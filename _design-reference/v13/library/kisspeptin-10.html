What it does

Kisspeptin-10 is the 10-amino-acid C-terminal fragment of kisspeptin, the neuropeptide that sits at the very top of the reproductive hormone cascade. It signals through the KISS1 receptor on GnRH neurons.

By driving endogenous GnRH release, it activates the entire HPG axis one step upstream of gonadorelin, raising LH, FSH, and downstream sex-hormone output.

Mechanism

Kisspeptin-10 activates the KISS1 receptor (KISS1R/GPR54) on hypothalamic GnRH neurons, stimulating the release of GnRH.

That endogenous GnRH pulse then drives pituitary LH and FSH secretion and downstream gonadal steroidogenesis — placing kisspeptin as the master upstream regulator of the reproductive axis.
