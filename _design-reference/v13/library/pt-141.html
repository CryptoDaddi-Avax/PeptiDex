What it does

PT-141 (bremelanotide) is a synthetic melanocortin-receptor agonist that acts centrally rather than on the vascular system. It is a metabolite-derived analog of melanotan-II selected for its effects on sexual arousal.

Unlike PDE5 inhibitors, it works upstream in the central nervous system through MC4R signaling, and is studied for hypoactive sexual desire disorder in both sexes.

Mechanism

PT-141 activates melanocortin receptors — principally MC4R — within central-nervous-system circuits that govern sexual arousal and motivation.

Because the effect originates centrally rather than through vasodilation, its pharmacology is distinct from PDE5 inhibitors and is dosed on demand ahead of activity.

Central arousal

Activates MC4R in CNS circuits governing sexual motivation, upstream of vascular effects.

Non-vascular mechanism

Distinct from PDE5 inhibitors; effect does not depend on direct vasodilation.

On-demand kinetics

Short half-life supports as-needed dosing ahead of anticipated activity.
