What it does

Glutathione (GSH) is a tripeptide of glutamate, cysteine, and glycine — the most abundant antioxidant produced inside human cells. It is central to neutralizing reactive oxygen species and to Phase II detoxification in the liver.

Because oral absorption is limited, research and clinical use favor IV or injectable delivery, where it has been studied for hepatic protection, detoxification support, and dermatological skin-lightening effects.

Mechanism

Glutathione directly scavenges reactive oxygen species and serves as the substrate for glutathione peroxidase, neutralizing peroxides. Its thiol (cysteine) group is the chemically active site.

In the liver it conjugates with xenobiotics during Phase II metabolism to make them water-soluble for excretion, and it regenerates oxidized vitamins C and E — anchoring the cellular antioxidant network.
