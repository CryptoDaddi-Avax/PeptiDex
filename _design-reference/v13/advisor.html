What's the half-life of Tesamorelin?
10:42 AM
Tesamorelin has a plasma half-life of approximately 26 minutes under repeated dosing (FDA Egrifta prescribing information). Despite rapid clearance, it sustains effects by triggering endogenous GH pulses operating on a longer biological timeline. FDA Egrifta JCEM 2007

Compare Retatrutide and Tirzepatide for body recomposition research.
10:43 AM
Both target incretin pathways with different mechanisms:
  • Retatrutide (GLP-1/GIP/glucagon triple-agonist): 24.2% body-weight reduction at 48 weeks in Phase 2 (Jastreboff et al., NEJM 2023)
  • Tirzepatide (GLP-1/GIP dual-agonist): 22.5% reduction at 72 weeks (SURMOUNT-1, NEJM 2022)
Retatrutide's glucagon arm appears to drive additional energy expenditure. Tirzepatide has more mature safety data. NEJM 2023 SURMOUNT-1 NEJM 2022
10:43 AM
Is BPC-157 documented to interact with Tirzepatide?
10:44 AM
No documented mechanistic conflicts in the published literature. BPC-157 acts on VEGFR2/angiogenesis pathways (PMID 14554208); Tirzepatide on GLP-1/GIP receptors. Both have been studied in separate research contexts. Researchers exploring combinations should coordinate injection timing to isolate injection-site reactions, which can occur with either. PMID 14554208 SURMOUNT-1
