Common questions

What is a peptide stack?

A peptide stack is a documented combination of peptides studied together for a shared research goal. PeptiDex stacks are research references, not medical protocols.

How does PeptiDex choose stacks?

Each combination is grounded in pharmacological rationale and at least three peer-reviewed studies per component, with synergies modeled in PK terms. See the methodology and disclaimers.

Which stack is most studied for fat loss?

The Cutting Stack is indexed for fat-loss research, pairing a GLP-1 agonist with lipolytic support. Take the 60-second quiz to match a stack to your interest.

Are PeptiDex stacks medical protocols?

No. They are documented research combinations describing published literature — not medical protocols, prescriptions, or recommendations for self-administration.
