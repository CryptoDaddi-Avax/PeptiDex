What does the Peptide Comparison do?

The Peptide Comparison tool builds a side-by-side attribute table for two or three peptides. It aligns the data that matters when choosing between closely related compounds — drug class, half-life, dose range, study count, headline efficacy, and lowest per-mg pricing — in a single view rather than across separate profile pages.

How do you use the Peptide Comparison?

Select two or three peptides and the tool assembles the comparison table from each compound's profile data. For the most common head-to-head questions, dedicated pages such as Retatrutide vs Tirzepatide expand the comparison into full prose with direct answers.

How is the Peptide Comparison different from the Evidence Dashboard?

The Peptide Comparison tool is for a focused head-to-head of a few named compounds, while the Evidence Dashboard ranks the entire library by strength of evidence. Use comparison when you've narrowed to two or three candidates; use the dashboard when you're still surveying the field.
