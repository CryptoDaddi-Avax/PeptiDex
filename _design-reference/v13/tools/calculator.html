What does the Reconstitution Calculator do?

The Reconstitution Calculator solves the arithmetic of turning a freeze-dried peptide into an injectable research solution. You enter the vial size in mg and the volume of bacteriostatic water added; it returns the resulting concentration, the dose contained in common insulin-syringe unit marks, and how many doses the vial provides.

How do you use the Reconstitution Calculator?

Enter the vial size (mg) and the BAC-water volume (mL). The calculator divides to give concentration, then maps that onto U-100 insulin-syringe units so you can read a target dose directly off the syringe. It appears inline on every peptide profile so the math is attached to each compound's dosing data.

How is the Reconstitution Calculator different from the Cycle Planner?

The Reconstitution Calculator handles a single vial's concentration and syringe-unit math, whereas the Cycle Planner handles the full multi-week schedule of doses. Use the calculator to prepare and measure each individual dose; use the planner to sequence those doses across a cycle.
