What does the Cycle Planner do?

The Cycle Planner converts a research protocol into a week-by-week dosing calendar. You enter each peptide, its dose, frequency, and cycle length, and the planner lays out the full schedule — including escalation steps and overlapping injection days — so a multi-component protocol can be read at a glance.

How do you use the Cycle Planner?

Add one or more peptides, set each dose, frequency, and cycle length, and the planner renders a combined timeline. It highlights weeks where doses escalate and where multiple injections fall on the same day, which informs site rotation. The schedule pairs with the Reconstitution Calculator to translate doses into syringe units.

How is the Cycle Planner different from the Reconstitution Calculator?

The Cycle Planner works at the level of the whole protocol — the sequence of doses across weeks — while the Reconstitution Calculator works at the level of a single vial, converting mg into BAC-water volume and syringe units. They are complementary: plan the cycle, then calculate each dose.
