What does the PK Plasma Curves do?

The PK Plasma Curves tool visualizes how a peptide's plasma concentration rises and falls over time. From a half-life and dosing interval it plots a one-compartment pharmacokinetic curve — showing a single dose's decay, or how repeated doses accumulate toward steady state — so the exposure pattern of a protocol can be seen rather than inferred.

How do you use the PK Plasma Curves?

Enter the half-life, dose interval, and timespan. The tool computes and plots the plasma curve, marking each dosing event and indicating when steady state is reached. The same engine renders the PK curve embedded in every peptide profile's Dosing section.

How is the PK Plasma Curves different from the Reconstitution Calculator?

PK Plasma Curves models concentration in the body over time — the pharmacokinetic picture — while the Reconstitution Calculator handles the bench math of preparing and measuring a dose. One describes what happens after administration; the other prepares the dose itself.
