CJC-1295 vs Ipamorelin

Peptidex Editorial Team — independent research, no vendor sponsorship·Updated May 29, 2026·Sources: 14+18 indexed studies

Quick answer

CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin are the classic GH pairing: CJC-1295 (a GHRH analog) raises the amplitude of GH pulses while Ipamorelin (a selective GHRP) triggers clean, additional pulses.

  • CJC-1295: Sustained GH elevation over 14 days (Teichman et al., JCEM 2006).
  • Ipamorelin: Selective GH release without cortisol elevation (Raun et al., Eur J Endocrinol 1998).
  • CJC-1295 half-life is 6–8 d (DAC) vs Ipamorelin at ~2 h; both administered SubQ.
  • Lowest per-mg pricing in the PeptiDex index: Ipamorelin at $0.95/mg.
