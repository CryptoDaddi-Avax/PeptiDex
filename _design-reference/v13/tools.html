Common questions

What research tools does PeptiDex offer?

PeptiDex offers eight free tools: cycle planner, reconstitution calculator, PK plasma curves, COA analyzer, evidence dashboard, peptide comparison, interaction checker, and price comparison.

Is the reconstitution calculator free?

Yes. All eight tools are free and require no account. They are methodology-reference tools that describe research calculations, not personal-use guidance.

What does the COA Analyzer do?

The COA Analyzer helps read and validate a Certificate of Analysis — confirming HPLC purity and mass-spec identity for a peptide batch.
