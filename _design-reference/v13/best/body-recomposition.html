§ RESEARCH GOALS / BODY RECOMPOSITION

Peptides most studied for body recomposition

Compounds researched for simultaneous fat loss and lean-mass preservation — incretin agonists paired with growth-hormone secretagogues.

Quick answer

Body recomposition research centers on combining an incretin agonist (Retatrutide or Tirzepatide) for fat loss with a growth-hormone secretagogue pair (CJC-1295/Ipamorelin) to preserve lean mass during a caloric deficit.

  • Retatrutide produced 24.2% mean body-weight reduction at 48 weeks in Phase 2 (Jastreboff et al., NEJM 2023).
  • Tirzepatide produced 22.5% reduction at 72 weeks in SURMOUNT-1 (NEJM 2022).
  • CJC-1295/Ipamorelin drives pulsatile GH release studied for lean-tissue support.
  • The Recomp Stack pairs these mechanisms over a documented 12-week research cycle.

What peptides are most studied for body recomposition?

The most-studied body-recomposition peptides are the incretin agonists Retatrutide and Tirzepatide for fat loss, paired with the growth-hormone secretagogues CJC-1295 and Ipamorelin for lean-mass preservation. The pairing reflects the core recomposition rationale: drive a caloric deficit while protecting lean tissue.

RetatrutideTriple agonist GLP-1/GIP/glucagon agonist with the largest body-weight reductions reported for an incretin agent. TirzepatideDual agonist GLP-1/GIP dual agonist with mature safety data, FDA-approved as Mounjaro/Zepbound. CJC-1295GHRH analog GHRH analog that sustains GH elevation, commonly paired with Ipamorelin. IpamorelinGHRP Selective ghrelin-receptor agonist that triggers clean pulsatile GH release.

How do these peptides work for body recomposition?

Each peptide contributes a distinct mechanism to the recomposition equation:

  • Retatrutide. Adds a glucagon arm to incretin agonism, which raises energy expenditure alongside appetite suppression — studied for fat loss with a metabolic-rate component.
  • Tirzepatide. Combines GLP-1 appetite suppression with GIP-driven insulin sensitivity, producing strong fat loss with more established tolerability data.
  • CJC-1295. Raises baseline growth-hormone output, studied alongside a deficit to support retention of lean tissue.
  • Ipamorelin. Stimulates GH pulses without raising cortisol or prolactin, the lean-mass-preservation half of the recomposition pairing.

Which research stacks target body recomposition?

The flagship combination is the Recomp Stack, which layers an incretin agonist with a GH-secretagogue pair and a healing peptide. For a simpler fat-loss-first approach, the Cutting Stack is the documented alternative.

  • The Recomp Stack — Retatrutide + CJC-1295/Ipamorelin + BPC-157 over a 12-week research cycle.
  • The Cutting Stack — A leaner, beginner-oriented fat-loss alternative when lean-mass support is secondary.
