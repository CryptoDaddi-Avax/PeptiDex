Peptides most studied for skin & aesthetic

Compounds researched for dermal renewal — collagen synthesis, expression-line softening, and cellular-age modulation.

Quick answer

Skin research centers on GHK-Cu for collagen synthesis and gene modulation, Argireline for topical expression-line reduction, and Epitalon for systemic cellular renewal — covering structural, muscular, and cellular angles.

  • GHK-Cu modulates over 4,000 human genes in skin fibroblasts (Pickart et al., 2015).
  • Argireline reduced wrinkle depth ~30% in topical study (Blanes-Mira et al., 2002).
  • Epitalon supports telomerase-linked cellular renewal.

What peptides are most studied for skin and aesthetic?

The most-studied skin-and-aesthetic peptides are GHK-Cu for collagen and gene modulation, Argireline for topical line reduction, and Epitalon for cellular renewal.

GHK-CuCopper peptide Copper-binding tripeptide central to skin remodeling research. ArgirelineCosmetic peptide Topical hexapeptide modeling botulinum-like muscle relaxation. EpitalonTelomerase Pineal tetrapeptide studied for cellular renewal and skin aging.

How do these peptides work for skin and aesthetic?

They address skin from three angles:

  • GHK-Cu. Delivers copper and activates dermal fibroblasts, stimulating collagen synthesis and a reparative gene-expression profile.
  • Argireline. Competitively inhibits SNAP-25 to soften the fine-muscle contraction that forms expression lines.
  • Epitalon. Induces telomerase activity linked to dermal cell renewal — the systemic, cellular-age angle of skin research.
