Peptides most studied for hormonal optimization

Compounds researched for endocrine support — HPG-axis stimulation and GH/IGF-1-axis support, an advanced research area.

Quick answer

Hormonal research targets the HPG axis with Gonadorelin and Kisspeptin-10, plus GH/IGF-1 support via Tesamorelin — restarting and supporting endogenous hormone production rather than replacing it.

  • Gonadorelin restores LH/FSH pulsatility via the pituitary GnRH receptor (Sykiotis et al., 2010).
  • Kisspeptin-10 produced robust LH and testosterone elevation in men (Dhillo et al., 2005).
  • Tesamorelin drives GHRH-mediated GH and IGF-1 support.
  • The Hormonal Reset combines these over an advanced 12-week cycle.

What peptides are most studied for hormonal optimization?

The most-studied hormonal-optimization peptides are Gonadorelin and Kisspeptin-10 for HPG-axis stimulation, plus Tesamorelin for GH/IGF-1-axis support. This is considered an advanced-methodology research area.

GonadorelinGnRH analog Synthetic GnRH decapeptide for HPG-axis support during research protocols. Kisspeptin-10Reproductive Decapeptide acting one step upstream of GnRH at the top of the axis. TesamorelinGHRH analog GHRH analog adding GH/IGF-1-axis support to the hormonal picture.

How do these peptides work for hormonal optimization?

Each acts at a different point in the endocrine cascade:

  • Gonadorelin. Pulsatile dosing activates the pituitary GnRH receptor to release LH and FSH, maintaining testicular function.
  • Kisspeptin-10. Stimulates KISS1R on GnRH neurons, triggering endogenous GnRH pulses and downstream sex-hormone output.
  • Tesamorelin. Stimulates physiological GH release, raising IGF-1 as the growth-axis complement to HPG support.
