## What is [Peptide Name]?

A 1–2 paragraph explainer. Cover: chemical class, peptide sequence length / source
species (if relevant), how it was discovered, why researchers became interested in it.
Cite the original characterization paper if available [1].

If the peptide is FDA-approved for any indication, state the indication and the
brand name(s) here, with a citation to the FDA label [2]. If not approved, state that
plainly: "Not approved by the FDA for human use; classified as a research compound."

## How [Peptide] Works (Mechanism of Action)

### Receptor binding and pathway

Describe the primary target — receptor name, downstream pathway, where it acts in the
body. Use plain language ("activates the GLP-1 receptor on pancreatic beta cells,
which increases insulin secretion in response to glucose") rather than dense
biochemistry. Cite the canonical mechanism review or original receptor-binding paper [n].

If the mechanism is contested or has multiple proposed pathways, say so.

### Pharmacokinetics summary

A short paragraph or compact table:

| Property | Reported value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Half-life (subcutaneous) | X hours | [n] |
| Time to peak plasma concentration | X hours | [n] |
| Bioavailability (route) | X% | [n] |
| Primary clearance route | renal / hepatic / proteolytic | [n] |
| Plasma protein binding | X% | [n] |

Where a value is debated or has been reported across a range, give the range and the
sources that bracket it.

## Dosing in Research Settings

> **Important.** The numbers below describe doses used in published research, not
> recommendations. Decisions about peptide use must be made with a licensed physician.

### Reported dosing ranges

For each route of administration with published data:

- **Subcutaneous.** In a [year] [trial type] (n=[N]), participants received [X] mg
  [frequency] for [duration] [n].
- **Oral.** [Same frame.]
- **Intramuscular.** [Same frame.]

Quote each cited study verbatim where possible: dose, frequency, duration, n.

### Half-life and timing

Restate the half-life from the PK table and explain what it implies for dosing
intervals **as observed in studies** — never as a recommendation. Example:
"The reported subcutaneous half-life of approximately X hours is consistent with the
once-weekly dosing used in the Phase 3 program [n]."

### Reconstitution

Lyophilized peptides require reconstitution before injection. Use the
[reconstitution calculator](/tools/calculator) to convert vial size, BAC water
volume, and target dose into mL-per-injection.

## Safety, Side Effects & Contraindications

### Reported adverse events

|---|---|---|---|

preclinical pharmacovigilance; comparable human safety data has not been published").

### Populations who should avoid

cancers, etc. Each restriction needs a source — the FDA label is the gold standard
where available [n].

citation. If interaction data is absent ("no formal drug-interaction studies have
been published for this compound"), say so.
