**Correction Notes from Plan:**
- **CORRECTION:** The original plan associated the "VEGFR2 upregulation, angiogenesis" finding specifically with the "Sikiric lab". While Predrag Sikirić is the pioneer of BPC-157 research and co-authors many of its papers, the primary study most often cited for the exact VEGFR2 internalization/activation mechanism was led by researchers at the Chang Gung Memorial Hospital in Taiwan (Hsieh, Huang, et al.). The drafting phase must cite the Hsieh 2017 paper when discussing VEGFR2, rather than broadly attributing that specific molecular mechanism discovery solely to the Sikirić lab.
