**Correction Notes from Plan:**
- **CRITICAL CORRECTION:** The original plan stated "17.5% trunk fat reduction in SEROSTIM Phase 3 trials". This is factually incorrect. Serostim is the brand name for somatropin (recombinant human growth hormone). Tesamorelin's brand name is EGRIFTA. 
- Furthermore, the 17.5% reduction figure specifically applies to **Visceral Adipose Tissue (VAT)**, not just general "trunk fat". The drafting phase MUST accurately cite VAT reduction from the Falutz Phase 3 pooled trials for Tesamorelin (Egrifta), completely omitting any reference to Serostim.
