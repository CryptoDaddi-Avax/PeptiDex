§ 04 — Editorial position

The field has doubled in a decade — but the signal-to-noise ratio has only gotten worse. We built PeptiDex because the science deserves better than forum anecdotes and affiliate fluff.

Synthetic peptides are short chains of amino acids that serve as foundational tools for understanding cellular signaling, molecular pathways, and systemic biological responses. When indexed carefully and sourced cleanly, they are one of the most exciting frontiers in research biology.

Our sourcing database strictly catalogs suppliers who publish transparent, third-party Certificates of Analysis — ensuring researchers have access to compounds exceeding 99% purity. We pull studies directly from PubMed and peer-reviewed journals. No supplements for sale. No human-use claims. Just the index.
